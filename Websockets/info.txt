What are the types of Communication in backend 

Synchronous(Strong Coupling )
1.HTTP (REST / GraphQL)
2.WebSocket (debatable)


Strong Coupling Mean:
1.Caller waits For Response 
2.If doenstram service fails - request fails 




Asynchronous (Weak Coupling)
1.Messaging queues
2.Pub/Sub
3.Server-Sent Events
4.WebSocket (can be async)


Weak Coupling Means:

Caller does NOT wait
Fire-and-forget
Systems loosely connected



Websockets 
WebSockets provide persistent, full-duplex communication over a single TCP connection.

Persistent → Connection stays open
Full-duplex → Both client and server can send anytime
Single TCP connection → No repeated handshakes


USE OF WEBSOCKETS IN  REAL LIFE 

1.Chat apps
2.Live sports updates
3.Real-time gaming
4.Financial tickers (example: Binance SOL/USDT)
5.ollaborative editing
6.Live customer support
